Signal Quality Model

Signal strength is calculated based on Friis transmission equation with terrain occlusion and D-layer ionospheric absorption.

Model Detail Breakdown
  • Path Loss (Friis): 1/d² power drop based on distance and 30-512MHz frequency scaling.
  • Terrain (LOD): 30m resolution SRTM sampling for Line-of-Sight occlusion check.
  • Ionosphere: D-layer absorption based on simulated solar hour and ionic density.
  • Clarity (SNR): Final audio fidelity penalized by thermal noise floor and distortion settings.
