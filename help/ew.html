Electronic Warfare (EW)

Simulate a contested electromagnetic environment via jamming and EMI.

1. Signal Degradation (EMI)
Injects background electromagnetic interference (static) onto specific channels. Operators will notice a higher noise floor, forcing them to speak louder or move closer to hear each other.
2. Area Jamming
A brute-force attack on a frequency. Instead of just static, Area Jamming causes the server to actively drop audio packets.
  • At 0.5 Intensity, voices become choppy and robotic.
  • At 1.0 Intensity, the channel is completely blacked out.
3. Targeted & EMCON
Surgical strikes against specific units.
  • Targeted Jamming: Hits specific callsigns only, simulating localized directional jammers.
  • EMCON: (Emission Control) Completely disables a unit's ability to transmit. Simulates strict radio silence orders or catastrophic hardware destruction.
Note: Use EW sparingly for maximum effect. Overuse can frustrate operators who aren't trained to switch to alternate channels under duress.
