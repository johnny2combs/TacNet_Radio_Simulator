After-Action Review (AAR)

Record and timestamp exercises for debrief analysis.

1. Starting a Recording
Give your session a name and hit Start Recording. TacNet will log every single PTT event, noting the callsign, duration, channel, and exact signal clarity score.
2. H-Hour Sync
Often, simulation software (like VBS4 or SWORD) runs on an artificial timeline (e.g., mission starts at 06:00:00). You can set the AAR clock to 'Manual H-Hour' so that your radio logs match the timestamps in your visual simulator perfectly.
3. SWORD Integration
If you use SWORD, you can paste the SimBridge URL here. TacNet will automatically synchronize its AAR clock with the live SWORD simulation time.
