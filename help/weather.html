Meteorological Simulator

Apply real-world weather patterns to degrade RF propagation.

1. Atmospheric Noise
Weather isn't just cosmetic in TacNet. Rain, snow, and dense clouds physically scatter RF waves and raise the electromagnetic noise floor. As weather worsens, maximum range drops exponentially.
2. Weather Profiles
  • Clear: Perfect RF conditions.
  • Light Rain: Marginal impact on VHF; slight static at maximum ranges.
  • Thunderstorm: Severe RF degradation. Handheld radios will struggle to communicate beyond visual range.
3. Global Impact
Changes applied here take 5-10 seconds to fully propagate through the physics engine, simulating a rolling weather front.
