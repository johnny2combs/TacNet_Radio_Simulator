Server Audio Monitor

Listen to live network traffic directly from the server.

1. Server Monitoring
This tool allows an administrator or instructor to 'jack in' to any channel and monitor voice traffic without needing to launch a dedicated client application.
2. Configuration
Select the channel you wish to monitor from the dropdown. Adjust the volume slider to your preference. You will hear all traffic exactly as the server receives it, prior to any destination physics degradation.
3. Split-Ear Spatial Audio & Diagnostics
The TacNet Client audio engine features advanced tactical spatial panning and decryption status signals:
  • Split-Ear Panning: When enabled, Primary Channel traffic pans entirely to the Left Ear, Monitored Channels pan entirely to the Right Ear, and unassigned/unknown signals mix in Both Ears at constant power ($0.707\times$). This enables multi-channel listening without audio clutter.
  • COMSEC Screech: If an incoming packet has mismatched crypto keys, the DSP engine immediately routes a vector-synthesized LFO-modulated digital screeching sound to the operator to diagnose a COMSEC mismatch.
Note: You cannot transmit from this page. To communicate with operators, you must run TacNet-Client.exe and authenticate with a valid Callsign.
