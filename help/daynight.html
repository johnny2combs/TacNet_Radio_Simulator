Solar & Ionospheric Engine

Simulate the time of day to model HF/VHF atmospheric absorption.

1. The D-Layer Effect
During daylight hours, solar radiation ionizes the D-Layer of the atmosphere. This acts like a sponge, absorbing low-frequency radio waves. In TacNet, Daytime significantly penalizes HF communications.
2. Nighttime Propagation
At night, the D-Layer dissipates, allowing radio waves to bounce off the higher layers of the ionosphere (Skywave). Switching the simulation to Night Mode will drastically increase the maximum effective range of your network.
3. Integration
This tool is primarily effective if you are utilizing HF/VHF hardware profiles. Shorter range UHF nets are largely unaffected by solar activity.
