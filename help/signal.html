2. State Tiers
  • Clear: Signal > 70%. Audio is perfect.
  • Degraded: Signal > 35%. Audio has noticeable static.
  • Intermittent: Signal > 10%. Audio is heavily distorted and dropping out.
  • Lost: Signal < 10%. Complete radio blackout.
