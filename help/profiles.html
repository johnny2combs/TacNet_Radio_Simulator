Radio Hardware Profiles

Define the physical RF capabilities of military communication devices.

1. The Physics Engine
TacNet doesn't just do basic voice chat; it simulates actual RF physics. A radio's profile dictates exactly how far and clear its transmissions travel. All active callsigns must be assigned a hardware profile.
2. Core RF Parameters
  • TX Power (Watts): The raw brute force of the transmission. 5W is typical for handhelds, 50W for vehicles.
  • Max Range (km): The absolute limit before the signal is entirely lost to thermal noise.
  • Full Quiet (km): The distance within which the signal remains crystal clear (high SNR). Beyond this, static slowly creeps in.
3. Advanced Modulation & Sensitivity
Unlike generic simulators, TacNet models specific modulations.
  • Sensitivity (dBm): How well the receiver hears weak signals. -120 dBm is much more sensitive than -100 dBm.
  • Frequency Range: Ensures operators cannot transmit out of bounds for their specific device.
  • SATCOM Power: Dedicated burst wattage used exclusively for orbital routing.
4. Applying Changes
1
Click Edit on a profile in the matrix.
2
Adjust the granular parameters in the advanced modal.
3
Click Save Profile. All clients using this hardware are updated in real-time.
Note: Changes take effect instantly. If you drop a handheld's power to 1W, operators currently talking miles apart will instantly hear their comms drop into heavy static.
